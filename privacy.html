Privacy Policy — Hermes Mail

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Hermes Mail is a private app used only by the owner of the connected Gmail account. It has no other users and is not offered to the public.

Google data accessed

The app requests the Gmail scopes gmail.readonly, gmail.send, and gmail.modify. It accesses message metadata (sender, subject, date, and snippet) and the message content needed to carry out actions requested by the owner, including reading, labeling or modifying, and sending email.

Use and storage

The app sorts and summarizes email for the owner and sends or modifies messages only at the owner's request. Message fields may be processed by the configured assistant classifier to produce those summaries. On a private server owned by the account owner, it persistently stores only message identifiers and synchronization cursors; message bodies are not persistently stored.

Sharing

Summaries are delivered to the owner's private Discord space. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or transferred to other third parties. It is not used to train general-purpose AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Hermes Mail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revocation and contact

The owner can revoke access at Google Account permissions. For privacy questions, open an issue in the repository.
